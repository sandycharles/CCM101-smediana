# Mission Reflection

Among the three major cloud platforms, **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** impressed me the most because of its wide range of services and flexibility. During my research, I learned that AWS provides services for almost every major cloud computing requirement, including EC2 for computing, S3 for storage, VPC for networking, and IAM for identity management. Its large ecosystem and mature cloud infrastructure make it a strong option for organizations ranging from startups to large enterprises.

AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform (GCP) have many similarities. All three provide essential cloud services such as virtual machines, object storage, networking, databases, and identity management. They also use flexible pricing models, operate infrastructure across multiple global regions, and support modern technologies such as containers and Kubernetes. In addition, all three platforms provide free or introductory options that allow beginners to explore their services.

Business requirements have a major influence on cloud platform selection. From the client scenarios in Checkpoint 4, I learned that the best platform depends on the organization's existing environment and goals. For example, the university was better suited to Azure because it already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory. On the other hand, the AI research company was better suited to GCP because of its strong AI/ML services, GPUs, and TPUs.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that recommending a cloud platform is not simply about choosing the most popular provider. A good recommendation requires matching technical capabilities, budget, scalability, security, and existing technologies to the client's specific business needs.

After completing this mission, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has also improved significantly. I now have more organized Markdown documentation, comparison tables, structured research files, screenshots as evidence, and clearer explanations of cloud services. This mission also helped me practice presenting technical information in a way that is easier for others to understand and use when making cloud technology decisions.
